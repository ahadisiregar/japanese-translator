# PROMPT: Design "Edition 1" of a Satirical Monopoly-Style Board Game

## Your role
You are a senior tabletop game designer and satirical writer. Design the complete first edition of an adult satirical board game. It should feel like Monopoly, but the goal is different: players compete to stay sane, not to become the richest.

## Core concept
- Title: use a placeholder title, [GAME TITLE], and offer 5 alternatives.
- Players: 3 to 6.
- Playtime: 60 to 90 minutes.
- Win condition: the last player whose Kewarasan (Sanity) is above 0 wins. Kewarasan is bilingual: "Sanity / Kewarasan".
- Languages: all card text and board tile names are bilingual, English first, then Bahasa Indonesia. Use natural Indonesian, including light everyday slang where it fits. Do not write stiff textbook Indonesian.
- Tone: sharp, dark, and sarcastic. It should be uncomfortable in the way true things are uncomfortable. It is for adults (17+) and not designed to be safe.

## Topics the game must cover
- General political issues. Political content must be in every edition, and this edition must include at least 30 political cards.
- Sarcasm and irony about modern life.
- Dark jokes and ugly truths about money, work, housing, healthcare, education, and status.
- Financial problems: debt, cicilan (installments), paylater, inflation, rent, gig work, side hustles, scams, investment hype.
- Modern life problems: doomscrolling, group chats, algorithms, hustle culture, a 9-to-5 life, traffic, burnout, loneliness, "sudah dewasa tapi belum mapan".
- Memes: text-only meme cards that describe a recognizable format or joke structure. Do not use copyrighted images or screenshots.

## Board: 40 tiles, Monopoly-style
Create the full list of 40 tiles with the tile name (EN / ID), type, and effect:
- Corner 1, GO: "Gajian / Payday". Collect Rp 2,000,000 each time you pass it. Landing here restores 1 Kewarasan.
- Corner 2: "Burnout Clinic / Klinik Burnout" (Jail). Visiting only; landing here as a "just visiting" stop has no penalty. Being sent here means skipping 2 turns and losing 1 Kewarasan.
- Corner 3: "Rest Stop / Istirahat Sejenak" (Free Parking). Rest here to restore 1 Kewarasan. No money is awarded for it.
- Corner 4: "Go to Overtime / Lembur Dulu" (Go to Jail). Move directly to Burnout Clinic and lose 2 Kewarasan.
- 22 Institution properties. Each is a real, publicly known type of institution: a bank, a platform, a ministry-type agency, a utility, a transport provider, a developer, a university, a hospital group, a retail chain, and so on. Name them by type, e.g., "Bank Besar (Big Bank)", with a fictional name if you prefer. Each property has a purchase price, rent, and a satirical description. Satire must rely on publicly known business models and public policies. Do not invent crimes or scandals and present them as fact.
- 4 Transit tiles, e.g., "KRL / Commuter Line" and "Ojol / Ride-hailing", with effects like "stuck in traffic: lose 1 Kewarasan".
- 2 Utility tiles, e.g., "PLN / Electricity", "Air / Water Bill", with fixed bills.
- 6 Card tiles (Kartu) that draw from the decks below.
- 2 Tax tiles: "Pajak Penghasilan / Income Tax" and "Pajak Hiburan / Entertainment Tax", with effects like "pay 10% of cash, or pay 2 Kewarasan".

Use this exact pattern for every tile in the table: Position | Name (EN / ID) | Type | Price | Rent / Effect | Flavor text.

## Economy and money
- Starting money: Rp 15,000,000 per player, plus 2 Kewarasan points. Start Kewarasan at 10 per player.
- Include a "Cicilan" (installment) mechanic. A player can borrow from the bank, but each loan adds a recurring Kewarasan drain each round until repaid.
- Include a "Paylater" mechanic: deferred debt that comes due on a random turn.
- Money can be lost, but it never directly causes elimination. Kewarasan is the only elimination mechanic.
- Explain how Kewarasan can be gained: rest tiles, good cards, passing GO, and playing "Self-Care" cards.

## Turn structure
Describe a complete turn in steps: roll, move, land, resolve, optional trade or build, debt check, and sanity check. Keep it under 150 words.

## Card decks: about 160 cards in total for Edition 1
Create each deck with this minimum count. Each card has: ID, Category, Title (EN / ID), Text (EN / ID), Effect (money, Kewarasan, movement, skip, or trade), and Flavor note.
- Politik / Politics: 30 cards. Cover general political issues: corruption, elections, public budgets, bureaucracy, subsidies, policy promises, lobbying, public services, and political hypocrisy. Balance the satire. Every side should be able to laugh at the system. Target incentives, systems, and institutions. Do not make one party or ideology the only target.
- Kenyataan Pahit / Ugly Truth: 25 cards. Blunt truths about money, work, and aging.
- Dark Joke: 25 cards. Dark humor about systems, mortality, debt, and modern anxieties. No cruelty toward protected groups, no jokes about tragedies that harm real victims, and no content involving minors.
- Sindiran / Sarcasm: 20 cards. Deadpan, ironic lines.
- Hidup Modern / Modern Life: 25 cards. Doomscrolling, group chats, KPIs, algorithms, the gig economy.
- Keuangan / Finance: 20 cards. Debt, inflation, investment hype, scams, rent, and cicilan.
- Meme: 15 cards. Text-only, describing a format or joke pattern.

## Real-world references
- Real institutions are allowed, but only as described in their public business models or documented public actions. Satire must be clearly framed as satire.
- Do not name real living politicians, celebrities, or private individuals.
- Do not write content that could be read as a real factual accusation against a real organization.

## Content guardrails (must follow)
- No slurs. No hateful content toward any race, religion, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, disability, or nationality.
- No content that encourages self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or drug use as a solution. Burnout and anxiety can be satirized, but not glamorized.
- No sexual content involving minors, and no sexual content that could be mistaken for it.
- No calls to real violence against any person or group.
- Satire should punch up at systems, incentives, and hypocrisy, not at people for who they are.

## Balance and playtesting
- Every card should have a clear, bounded effect. Keep sanity swings between -3 and +2 per card.
- Target a typical game length of 60 to 90 minutes.
- Include a playtest checklist: 10 items to test, such as "Does one player snowball early?", "Is any tile never landed on?", and "Are political cards too one-sided?"

## Output format
Produce the complete edition as a single document with these sections:
1. Title and tagline (EN / ID)
2. Components list (board, tokens, cards, bank notes, Kewarasan tracker, dice)
3. Setup
4. Rules: turn structure, Kewarasan, cicilan, paylater, win condition
5. Board table: all 40 tiles
6. Card tables: all decks, with the minimum counts above
7. Balance notes
8. Playtest checklist
9. Content note: confirm the guardrails were followed

Use tables for the board and cards. Use short paragraphs for the rules. Do not use bullet points for the rules section. Keep the writing sharp, not padded.

## Calibration examples (tone only, do not copy)

- ID: "Cek aplikasi investasi untuk ke-14 kalinya hari ini. Tidak dapat apa-apa. Kewarasan -1."

## Final check before you finish
Confirm the following, then state the confirmation in the document:
- Board: 40 tiles, all four corners set.
- Card decks: total around 160, Politik at 30 or more.
- Every card is bilingual.
- Kewarasan is the only elimination mechanic.
- Political content is balanced and does not single out real living people.
- Content guardrails are met.
